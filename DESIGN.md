# Design System: Zugig

This is direction #3, "Verre", written down for the agents picking up screen tickets. Ground truth is the shipped code: tokens in `app/src/main/kotlin/dev/betteriptv/app/ui/theme/Tokens.kt` (`Colors`, `Type`, `Corners`, `Spacing`, `Cards`, `FocusScale`), the theme and scrolling in `NuageTheme.kt`, components in `ui/components/`. Screens read tokens, never raw values.

**Units.** The app draws on a 960 x 540 dp canvas (1920 x 1080 at density 320). Every `px` in the frontmatter is a dp (or sp, for type) on that canvas, read from about 3 m.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Artwork Is the Interface"**

Verre is the Apple TV app (tvOS Liquid Glass) on a Fire TV. A Title's artwork fills the screen, edge to edge, and the interface floats on it as white type and translucent white glass. The interface brings no colour of its own. The ground is near-black, controls are glass capsules, posters have softly rounded corners, and the one solid white thing on screen is what the remote will act on.

The world refuses the category default: no grey streaming grid with a coloured accent, no Netflix black-and-red. Density is low on the browsing screens (one featured Title big, rows below) and tabular on details, where every Version of a Title lines up by language so the household can compare copies at a glance. Honesty about each Version is visual: a Confirmed attribute is a solid badge, a Likely one is an outlined badge, everywhere.

The Fire TV Stick is weak, so the glass is a flat translucent tint, never a live blur, and every effect is cheap: a scale, a shadow, a gradient, a border.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black ground; white and translucent white glass (12% and 20%) for everything that isn't artwork.
- Full-bleed backdrop behind the hero and details, held readable by scrims.
- Inter throughout; bold, tightly tracked display.
- Capsule controls, 12 dp poster corners, 4 dp badges.
- White fill means focus. Focused artwork lifts 1.12x with a deep shadow, a light sheen and a bright rim.
- A focused poster grows into its Title's textless horizontal artwork, named by its logo, as on Netflix.
- A card's name and badges appear only under the focused card.
- Lists scroll only when focus nears an edge (EdgeScroll).

## Colors

Achromatic by design: black ground, white ink, white glass. Colour on screen comes from artwork, plus amber for cautions and red for failures.

### Primary
- **Focus White** (`focus`): the fill of whatever has focus (buttons, tabs, keys, choices, settings rows), with `on-focus` black text on it. It is the app's only accent.

### Neutral
- **Near-Black Ground** (`ground`): behind everything, and the colour every scrim fades artwork into.
- **Picture Black** (`picture`, `#000000`): only behind the player's picture, on both engines (the player screen, Media3's shutter and view, VLC's layout), so letterbox bands are pure black (#273). Everywhere else, the player's scrims included, keeps `ground`: on a TV with HDR or Dolby Vision output the UI's near-black can be lifted to dark grey, which shows in the bands.
- **Glass** (`glass`, white at 12%): the resting surface of rows, fields, keys and the tab bar. Translucent, so artwork shows through.
- **Strong Glass** (`glass-high`, white at 20%): unfocused buttons, the selected tab, the selected choice, and focused Version rows and text fields.
- **Ink** (`ink`): primary text, the text cursor, card progress bars.
- **Muted Ink** (`ink-muted`, about 8.6:1 on the ground): secondary text such as metadata lines, column headings, setting details and current values. It also marks a disabled button's label.

### Certainty
- **Confirmed Fill** (`confirmed`) with **On-Confirmed** black text: a Confirmed badge. It is slightly off-white so it never reads as a focused control.
- **Likely Outline** (`likely`): the 1.5 dp outline and text of a Likely badge.

### Status
- **Caution Amber** (`caution`): cautions that aren't failures, such as a CAM copy or an Audio-description Version. On the details screen it is the colour of the note under a Version's badges.
- **Failure Red** (`error`): a failed login or Sync message, as text only.

### Named Rules
**The Artwork Carries the Colour Rule.** The interface adds no hue. The only non-neutral tokens are Caution Amber and Failure Red, and they appear as small text, never as fills, tabs or highlights. A team's colours count as its artwork (#349): a Game card's glass and a Game page's backdrop carry the two teams' colours from the schedule, as a Title's card and page carry its artwork; they never tint a control, a row or focus.

**The White Means Focus Rule.** No control or row is ever filled solid white unless it has focus. Three small indicators are exempt: the white progress bar on a card and on an Episode row, and the off-white `confirmed` badge.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter 700
**Body Font:** Inter 400 and 600
**Label/Mono Font:** Inter 600 (no second family)

**Character:** One family, three weights (400, 600, 700, bundled as `inter_*.ttf`). The display size is bold and tracked tight so a Title's name reads like a poster credit. Everything else is plain and even, built for reading from a couch. The sizes were set on the Fire TV Stick itself, close to tvOS and Material for TV on the same canvas: the earlier scale, estimated from photos of Netflix, read too big on the TV (#186), and then a step smaller again, as it still read too big on a 4K TV (#223).

**Text size.** The sizes here and in the frontmatter are **Medium**, the default. Settings > Display > Text size also offers **Small**, Medium × 0.9 (not rounded), and **Large**, the scale before #223 (about Medium × 1.1: display 42/44, headline 15/20, title 14/19, body, rail and rail-strong 14/20, rail-meta 12/17, detail and label 13/18, caption 11/15). Medium is Large × 0.9 rounded to whole sp, line heights in proportion. TV resolution isn't why: the app draws on a 960 × 540 dp canvas, so text takes the same share of a 1080p and a 4K screen, and Android can't reliably tell a TV's size in inches; the setting covers different TVs and viewing distances. The size is one `TypeScale`, provided by `NuageTheme` through `LocalTypeScale` and read by every `Type.*` style, so it applies everywhere at once with no restart. Only text scales: spacing, card sizes and the canvas don't, nor does the Top row numeral (`Type.numeral`), which is sized to the poster, nor the Wordmark (18 sp) and the login screen's 38 sp name.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (`Type.display`): a Title's name in the home hero and at the top of its details page. Two lines at most. A Title with a TMDB logo (in the App language, else English) shows the logo there instead (`TitleName`): fitted in a 320 × 88 dp box, the room two Display lines take, and sitting on its bottom edge, with the name as its content description. The box stays empty while the logo loads, and the name shows in Display type when there is no logo or it can't be loaded.
- **Headline** (`Type.headline`, 14 sp): row headings ("Continue watching"), screen and section headings ("Versions", "Playback"), the player's Title name. Kept modest so the posters under a heading lead, as on Netflix (#147). The Wordmark uses it at 18 sp.
- **Title** (`Type.title`): a Title's name under a focused card, the language group heading over Versions ("English · 3"), the search query.
- **Body** (`Type.body`, 13 sp): descriptions, metadata lines, people, messages, field text: what is read from the couch. Descriptions are capped at 2 lines in the hero and 3 on details.
- **Rail** (`Type.railMeta` 11 sp, `Type.rail` 13 sp, `Type.railStrong` 13 sp bold): the info under Home's focused rail (#182). It is no bigger than body text: a step above body read as the biggest text on the screen, ahead of the artwork, on a 4K TV (#223). So the description and the Continue watching lines are body's size in 600, the metadata line a step smaller and muted, and the time left in Continue watching strongest by weight alone.
- **Detail** (`Type.detail`, 12 sp): body beside label type in a row or table: Version cells and bitrate, setting values. It stays at the size of label type, so the French strings still fit the Version columns.
- **Label** (`Type.label`): buttons, tabs, choices, keys, setting labels, the Source cell of a Version row.
- **Caption** (`Type.caption`): badges, column headings, field labels, explanations under a setting, the Sync status line, the caption under Home's rows while a first Sync reads the Titles and the failure line under a checklist step.

### Named Rules
**The Readable From the Couch Rule.** All text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast against what is actually behind it. That includes glass over artwork, so for example inactive tab labels use white at 85%, not `ink-muted`. Nothing goes smaller than caption (10 sp at Medium, 9 sp at Small).

**The French Is Longer Rule.** French strings run about 20% longer. Fixed widths (Version columns, the settings label column, buttons in a row) must hold the French string, and single-line text gets `maxLines` with an ellipsis instead of overflowing.

## Layout

**Overscan-safe margins.** Every screen keeps its content inside `screen-x` (48 dp) left and right and `screen-y` (27 dp) top and bottom. Scrolling lists apply the margin as `contentPadding` rather than outer padding, so cards scroll under the edge and are never clipped while focused. Login doubles the horizontal margin (96 dp) to centre a two-column form.

**The shell.** `TopTabs` runs across the top inside the margins: the Wordmark on the left and the tab capsule centred. Tabs are Home, Movies, Shows, Live, then Search and Settings as icons. The tabs stay on screen on Home, Movies, Shows, Live and Search: the cards scroll beneath them and pass under their bottom edge. Picking a tab keeps focus on it, so Left and Right go on along the tabs and Down goes into the screen; Home's first card, Search's keyboard and Settings' first row take focus only when the screen is reached otherwise, as on launch. Up into the tabs lands on the selected one, not the nearest (#187). Back from any card focuses the selected tab and leaves the scroll position alone; Down from the tab returns to the card that had focus. Back on a tab does what it always did: Search goes to Movies, the other tabs leave the app.

**Home.** Home opens on a featured Title (`FeaturedHero`, #348). Its textless backdrop fills the screen behind the tabs and the rows, under the usual scrims, and fades in as it arrives like any artwork. The hero text column (440 dp wide) starts 96 dp below the tabs, at the bottom-left third. In order: its logo (`TitleName`), a metadata line (year · runtime · genres in muted body) followed by its badges, two lines of description, then Play and Details buttons 12 dp apart. The row after it peeks at the bottom. Play takes first focus. Play starts what the Title page's Play would (a Movie from where the Viewer stopped, a Show's Episode to watch next), and with nothing to play opens the Title page, which says why; Details opens the Title page. Back from either comes back to the button that opened it, on the same Title. The Library picks the Title (`Library.featured`) from the top five of Top movies and Top shows, taken in turn (a movie first), as the Top rows show them, skipping Titles in Continue watching and Titles without a textless backdrop or a logo. Each time the screen is entered (by its tab, or as the app starts) it features the next of them after the one featured last, kept on the device, so the same Title doesn't come back on the next entry. Coming back from a page it opened is not entering. The Title for the next entry is read during this one, its backdrop and logo with it, so it shows at once with the rows kept from last time. The first entry reads it beside the rows, and its backdrop and logo as soon as it is read, and the hero arrives whole (#386): the rows wait for the Title and its art up to `Motion.pageArrivalMillis` past their own reading, then show with the hero: the rows and the hero (its text and its backdrop together) fade in as one over `Motion.screenFadeMillis`, over the placeholder rows, which go once they have (#398). Never the hero's text alone before its picture, nor the rows under an empty hero. Art slower than that fades in as it comes, as any image does; a Title read later than that is kept for the next entry, and the screen opens on its first card. The hero never changes while the Viewer is on the screen, and never advances by itself: rows read again around it leave it alone. Without a Title that qualifies, there is no hero, and the screen opens on its first card as before. The first Down from the hero scrolls it fully off screen, under the tabs, so the first row's heading comes up to the top, and the backdrop goes with it, drawn at how much of the hero is still on screen. While focus rests on the hero, the backdrop the first row's first card grows into is loaded into memory, so that Down grows straight into its art (#398). Up from the first row to the hero brings it back whole, focus on the button focused there last. Up from the hero goes to the tabs. Back from the hero, as from any card, moves focus to the selected tab without scrolling, and Down from the tab comes back to where focus was. Past it, the info under the focused rail (`RailInfo`, below) does the hero's job, as on Netflix (#182). Artwork stays still: no video previews. The rows start 16 dp under the tabs: each a headline heading, 12 dp, then a `LazyRow` with `screen-x` horizontal and 10 dp vertical content padding, then its info 12 dp under it (every row keeps the slot, filled only under the focused row, so nothing moves down when the info arrives), then 14 dp before the next heading. The rows move like Netflix's, so focus stays in one place (#167): the focused card sits in the first slot at the left margin, and Right and Left scroll its row under it (`glideTo`, #341: the rail slides so the focused card starts at the margin, on the same 160 ms fast-out, slow-in curve as the cards' grow and shrink (`Motion.focus`), so Left and Right feel the same. It aims at where the card will sit once the card before it is a poster again, not where it is while that card still shrinks, and reads that aim again each frame, so the card lands on the margin exactly once: it never passes it and never settles back). A focused poster grows into its backdrop to the right (Cards, below), so the cards after it move right and the focused card stays at the margin. A rail with more cards than the screen shows at once (more than 7 posters, 6 Channels or 4 stills, counting the one peeking in the left margin; `RailLoop`; a grown card at the margin leaves room for only 6, so a looping poster rail still never shows a card twice) loops: Right on its last card goes on to its first and Left on its first back to its last, with no end either way, and the card before the focused one peeks in the margin. A shorter rail stops at its ends, where Left and Right go nowhere (not up to the tabs), but its last cards also scroll to the left margin, leaving room after them, so the focused card always sits above the info. A Top row (`RankedCard`, below) never loops, whatever its length: a ranked list has a start and an end, so Left on its first card and Right on its last go nowhere, and its last card too scrolls to the margin with room after it. Its card starts with the numeral, so the numeral sits at the margin and the focused poster right of it, growing to the right while the numeral stays put. Down and Up bring the next row's heading to the top of the rows, right under the tabs, and the rows above pass out of sight; the info moves under it as it slides, into the slot every row keeps, so the rows below never shift. The row after the focused one peeks at the bottom. Each row keeps its own scroll, also while a Title's details are open, and Down into a row returns to the card focused there last. A row entered for the first time, from the tabs or from the row above or below, starts at the card at the left margin, not the one nearest where focus came from: its first card, rank 1 on a Top row, or on a looping rail the one after the card peeking in the margin (#222). Posters (2:3, 136 dp) fill most rows. Continue watching uses 16:9 stills as tall as the posters (363 dp, #221) with a progress bar, so the Viewer sees where they stopped: Movies and Shows, last played first, a Show at the Episode to watch next. Right after it, only while a Game in the Viewer's Leagues is in progress and has a Feed, comes Live now: the Live tab's `GameCard`s (Live, below), in its order, with no name slot, the info under the rail naming the Game; with no such Game there is no row at all. Its Games are asked for when Home opens and every 2 minutes while the row is on screen, as on Live, but never hold the other rows: the Games kept on the device show first and the rows are read again once the cloud answers (the request is given 3 s), so a slow network costs Home nothing. OK on a Game opens its page; Back from it returns focus to its card, and if the Game ended meanwhile, to the card now in its place or the row that takes its place, like a removed Favourite (#256). Then come the Charts and the browse rows, in order: Top movies and Top shows in the Region (`RankedCard`s), New on your service, and Trending this week, a plain poster rail that loops when long. Favourites follow, as `ChannelCard`s in the order they were added, and the row is left out while there are none; the genre rows come last. Removing a Favourite moves focus to its neighbour, or to the row that takes its place. Back from a Title returns focus to the card that opened it, in the same row even when rows above it came or went. If that card has gone (the Title left Continue watching meanwhile), focus goes to the card now in its place, or to the row that takes its place, like a removed Favourite. Cards sit `card-gap` (20 dp) apart, enough that a 1.12x focused poster, or a 1.08x focused still or grown card (14.5 dp each side), never touches its neighbours. Home is never blank under the tabs (#213): until its rows are first read, two rows of `glass` posters (`PlaceholderRows`) hold their places, with no headings, focus or motion. Until a first Sync lands the Channels, its checklist (`SyncChecklist`) shows centred there instead, as on Live, Movies and Shows: a headline title ("Setting up your catalog") over one body line per step (Channels, Movies, Shows, Saving), each with a 10 dp dot before it: `ink` filled when done, a 1.5 dp `ink-muted` ring while pending, failure red when failed, and for the step running, the breathing `PulsingDot` (the one motion on the screen) with an ellipsis after its name. No counts: the list is the progress. A failed step names why under it in caption failure red, with a Retry `NuageButton` that takes focus. Once the Channels have landed, Home shows its rows (Live now, Favourites) and, under them, the same placeholder rows for the Title rows to come, captioned "Movies and shows arrive once syncing finishes" (caption, muted), until the Titles land; they then fill in place. Rows never move under focus (#342), on Home, Movies, Shows and Live alike: when the rows are read again while the Viewer is on one (Live's 2-minute Games refresh, Live now coming or going, a Sync landing), the cards already showing update their captions in place ("Live", a start time), rows above and below come and go without moving the focused row or its card on screen, and a change to the focused row itself (a card coming, going or moving) waits until focus leaves the row, up to the tabs or to another row; entering the screen again shows the rows as read. A looping rail knows its cards by their Title (or Game, Channel…) and copy, not by place, so a rail read again with other cards never shows one card's art in another's place.

**Movies and Shows.** The same rows as Home (`RowsScreen`), starting 16 dp under the tabs as Home's do (Sync now, the Sync status line and the Title counts are in Settings' Catalog section): the kind's Top row in the Region (`RankedCard`s, never looping), "Trending movies this week" (or shows), then "New movies" (or "New shows"), with a row per genre to follow (#200). Each tab shows a Title once on its own, so a Title on Home's Top row is on the tab's too. Each tab opens on its own featured Title, as Home does, picked from the top five of the kind's Top row, rotating on its own. There is no grid: Titles not in a row are found through Search. While the first Sync runs, its checklist shows centred where the rows will be, from Movies on (Movies, Shows, Saving: the Channels are Live's business); if it fails with no Titles stored, the failed step names why there in the error colour, with Retry. Until a tab's rows are first read, the same placeholder rows as Home's hold their place.

**Live.** The sports hub (M3), in Home's rows (`RowsScreen`), starting 16 dp under the tabs as Home's do, with the same focus, scroll and Back behaviour. Its rows, in order: Live now (the Games in progress that have a Feed), Today (the Games still to start today, by the device's clock), one row per League on (in Settings' order, its Games of the week ahead; a League with no Game in seven days gets no row; its heading carries the League's logo before its name, 20 dp, whole, `s` before the name, so the row is no taller than any other, and the name alone while the logo hasn't loaded or can't), then More live events, then Replays, then Favourites and Channels (the Channel browser's countries). More live events holds the Event channels no Game matched (tennis, snooker, handball, college games no schedule knows), whose name gives a start within six hours of now either way, by start, as `ChannelCard`s with no name slot: the info under the rail names the Event channel ("Djokovic vs Alcaraz", `rail`) over, muted, when it starts in the device's zone and clock as a Game card says it ("7:00 PM", "Tue 7:00 PM"), the name's time read in the Provider's zone (the Prefix table's event time zone) or the zone the name states; "Live" is never claimed, since the name's time is all that is known. OK watches it at once as any Channel, its best variant first; the menu key favourites it. Finished Event channels and empty slots are Clutter and never show; the row is left out when empty. A League's logo is its own, never a Game's: ESPN's dark-background league logo (the icon ESPN's pages show where it has none, as for the CFL), fixed per League in core. A Game shows once on the tab, in the highest row it fits, each row by start; a live Game with no Feed is hidden, an upcoming one with none shows disabled. The Game rows are `GameCard`s (below) with no name slot: the info under the rail names the Game. OK on a Game opens its page (below); Back from it returns to its card. Replays are the Sports replays (CONTEXT.md) whose two teams are teams of the Leagues on, newest first by the date in their names, then by when the Provider added them, at most 40, as `GameCard`s with both logos and the date as the caption ("Nov 9", with the year when it isn't this year's); the info under the rail names the League and both teams as a Game's ("NHL · Bruins at Maple Leafs") and, muted, the date in full ("November 9, 2025"). The row is left out when no Replay matches; the rest of the Sports replays, and highlights shows, are reached through Search. A Replay never shows a poster: its TMDB ID is wrong and ignored. The Games are asked for when the tab opens and every 2 minutes while a Games row is on screen, the kept copy standing when the cloud fails; the rows are read again on each ask, and never move under focus (Home, above): a focused Game that goes live says "Live" on its card where it is, and stays in its row, as one that finished does, until focus leaves the row; Live now appearing or leaving above moves nothing on screen. Favourites follow as `ChannelCard`s in the order they were added, with the menu key adding or removing the Favourite and focus moving to its neighbour, as on Home, or to the tab when no row is left (the move Back makes), so focus is never nowhere. ### Named Rules
**The Still Screen Rule.** Lists don't move on every press. Don't override `LocalBringIntoViewSpec`, and don't scroll to a pivot. Rely on EdgeScroll. A list may open somewhere other than its top once, when the screen opens (the Episode picker opens on the next Episode); from then on only EdgeScroll moves it. Home is the one exception: its rows keep focus at one place, the left margin and the top of the rows, and move under it on every press, as Netflix's do, with the focused row's info under it and its long rails looping.

**The Overscan Rule.** Nothing readable or focusable sits outside 48 x 27 dp from the screen edge at rest. Only artwork and scrims go full bleed.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from three things: artwork behind, translucent glass on top, and one lift for focused artwork. Glass is flat translucent white; there is no blur anywhere in the app. The only shadow belongs to a focused card.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card lift** (Compose `shadow(28.dp)`, black ambient and spot, roughly `0 14px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` in CSS): only under a focused `TitleCard`, drawn by `FocusSurface` with `FocusWeight.Card`.
- **Sheen** (linear gradient from white at 30% to transparent at 45% of the diagonal): the light catching a lifted card, drawn over its artwork.
- **Rim** (1.5 dp border, white at 60%): keeps a focused card visible where the shadow disappears into the dark ground.

### Backdrop and scrims
`Backdrop` (in `ui/components/`) crops the artwork to fill the screen, then lays two scrims of `ground` over it:
- **Horizontal:** 85% at the left, 25% at 55% of the width, transparent at the right. The hero text sits on the dark side.
- **Vertical:** 85% at the top (under the tabs), transparent from 20% to 35%, 85% at 75% of the height, solid `ground` at the bottom. Rows sit on the dark band.

The player uses its own vertical scrim, darker at the top (85%, then 60% at 12%) and 85% at the bottom.

When glass sits on busy artwork and needs to carry text, as in the player's buffering suggestion, it first lays `veil` (`ground` at 75%) and then `glass` over it. A screen of a Title's own over its backdrop (the Versions screen) lays `veil-deep` (`ground` at 85%, `Colors.veilDeep`) over the whole picture, the darkest a veil goes: muted caption text keeps 4.5:1 on it over white.

The player's Versions lay one `veil` under the whole list instead of one under each row: a slab with `card` top corners behind the headline row, `VersionHeader` and rows, `m` wider than the rows on each side, as tall as the Versions need up to 62% of the screen, and running off the bottom edge. Rows on separate veils would leave the moving picture flickering in the gaps between them, and the muted column names above the rows would stay on the thin top of the scrim. A Title's Versions sit on their own screen under `veil-deep` (Details, above).

### Named Rules
**The Tint, Not Blur Rule.** Glass is a translucent tint (`glass`, `glass-high`), not runtime blur. The Fire TV Stick can't afford a blur, and focus moves must stay instant.

**The One Lift Rule.** Only focused artwork lifts. Buttons, tabs, keys and rows never cast a shadow.

## Shapes

Corners are soft everywhere and there are no sharp rectangles. Every control is a capsule. A 40 dp button with `control` (22 dp) corners reads as a pill, and the tab bar and choices use fully rounded ends. Posters, stills and text fields have `card` (12 dp) corners. Badges are small rounded rectangles (`badge`, 4 dp) so they read as labels, not buttons. Version rows and setting rows take `control` corners, which makes them long pills. Artwork is always clipped to its corner shape, and the focus rim follows the same shape.

## Motion

Motion is short, and it's there so nothing pops, cuts or jumps on the Fire TV Stick. Every duration comes from `Motion` (`ui/theme/Tokens.kt`); screens never hold raw milliseconds.

- **Focus** (`Motion.focusMillis`, 160 ms, a fast-out, slow-in `tween`, `Motion.focus`): the focus scale, a focused poster growing into its backdrop or shrinking back, and its rail sliding the focused card to the left margin with it (#341), so the card lands once.
- **Image fade** (`Motion.imageFadeMillis`, 150 ms): artwork that arrives from disk or the network fades in from transparent, as alpha on the image alone, so nothing else redraws (`rememberArtwork`, used by every screen's card art, logos, stills and backdrops). Artwork already in the memory cache shows on its first frame with no fade, so going back to a screen, or scrolling back to a card, stays instant. A card's artwork still waits until the card has been on screen 250 ms (`SettleFirst`) before it loads; the fade starts once it arrives.
- **Fades never skip** (`Fade`): the image fade and the rows' arrival move on frame by frame, by at most 33 ms a frame, so a fade still shows over several frames after the main thread is held up (a cold start on the stick drew no frame for half a second, and a fade timed by the clock alone had finished by the next one, #398).
- **Page arrival** (`Motion.pageArrivalMillis`, 300 ms): the longest a page waits for its content before it appears, so it arrives whole and fades in together rather than assembling piece by piece. The rows screens (Home, Movies, Shows, Live) use it as their wait: bare under the tabs until it passes, then the pulsing dot (Components) centred in the room under the tabs, fading in over the screen fade; it crossfades out as the rows fade in. Rows that land within the wait, most entries', show no dot at all.
- **Screen fade-in** (`Motion.screenFadeMillis`, 150 ms, a linear-out, slow-in `tween`, #345): every change of screen in the app's navigation (a tab, a Title page, a Game page, a country's Channels, back again) and every Settings list opening or closing under the tabs fades the new screen in over the `ground`. The old screen is gone at once, on the new one's first frame: never a cross-dissolve, which would draw two screens at once on the stick. It's a `ground` veil over the new screen (`ScreenFade`), opaque on its first frame and thinning to nothing, so the screen is never drawn offscreen and only the veil redraws while it fades. The veil takes no focus or keys: focus lands where it always has, on the fade's first frame, and the Viewer can move on before the fade ends. The player opens and closes as a cut, as before: no fade over the picture, and none on the screen it returns to. A Title page opened from a grown card opens as a cut too, on that card's backdrop, and fades its own content in (Details, below).
- **Picture fade** (`Motion.screenFadeMillis`, 150 ms): the player's starting cover fades to the picture on its first rendered frame, on both engines (`StartingCover`, Layout above).

### Named Rules
**Arrive, Don't Pop.** Anything that appears after the screen is up (an image, a page's content, a screen) fades in over the `ground` or the glass under it, in one of the `Motion` durations above, instead of snapping in. Artwork the Viewer has already seen (in memory) shows at once: an image's fade is for arriving, never for coming back. A screen changing, Back included, always fades in, in the short screen fade, so a cut never reads as a glitch; only the player cuts. Focus is never delayed by a fade. A wait shows nothing for one beat, then the dot; never a spinner, a bar or a grey shape of what is coming, which can't promise the layout that arrives (Home opens on a hero and wide stills, Live on Game cards).

## Components

All focus feedback comes from `FocusSurface(weight = …)`. Never draw focus by hand.

### Focus grammar
- **`FocusWeight.Card`** (artwork): scales to 1.12x (a Continue watching still or a grown card, being wide, to 1.08x, so it stays clear of its neighbours) over 160 ms (`Motion.focusMillis`), lifts on the card shadow, gains the sheen and rim. The container doesn't change.
- **`FocusWeight.Control`** (buttons, tabs, keys, choices): scales to 1.06x over 160 ms and the container turns `focus` white with `on-focus` black content.
- **`FocusWeight.Row`** (anything that spans a list): does not scale, so its edges stay inside the overscan margin. Choose by content:
  - **White fill** when the row holds only label, detail and value text (`SettingRow`). All text switches to `on-focus`.
  - **White ring** (2 dp `focus` border on `glass-high`) when the row holds badges or several columns whose colours carry meaning (`VersionRow`, Movies list rows, `NuageTextField`), so the badges and muted cells stay readable.

### Buttons (`NuageButton`)
- **Shape:** capsule, 40 dp tall, 18 dp horizontal padding, optional 20 dp leading icon 8 dp before the label.
- **Rest:** `glass-high` with `ink` label.
- **Focus:** `focus` fill, `on-focus` label and icon, 1.06x.
- **Disabled:** stays glass with an `ink-muted` label, still focusable, and ignores the click.

### Action list (`PrimaryAction`, `ActionRow`)
- A details page's actions, stacked as rows of one width, each 40 dp tall with a 20 dp leading icon 8 dp before a label-type label, 18 dp in from the start.
- **`PrimaryAction`:** `glass-high` capsule at rest, naming what it plays. Its progress, when there is any, is a 48 x 4 dp bar after the label: `ink` over `ink` at 30%, `on-focus` over `on-focus` at 30% when focused. Disabled as a `NuageButton`.
- **`ActionRow`:** no container at rest, just the white icon and label. An optional value follows the label, `m` after it, in label type, `ink-muted` at rest and `on-focus` at 60% when focused (Versions' "English · 1080p · 10 Versions"); the label never shrinks for it, the value is cut with an ellipsis. Disabled as a `NuageButton`.
- **Focus:** `FocusWeight.Row` with a white fill, so neither row grows and the list keeps its width.

### Icon buttons (`NuageIconButton`, `NuageIcons`)
- **Shape:** a `glass-high` circle, 44 dp by default, holding a white icon half its size. `slot` centres a smaller button in a bigger one's height so a row lines up.
- **Focus:** `focus` fill, `on-focus` icon, 1.06x, and the button's name in caption `ink` 8 dp under it. The caption slot is always reserved, so nothing moves as focus does.
- Only for the player's transport, where the icons are universal. Anything less obvious keeps its words (a `NuageButton` with a leading icon).
- Icons are Material Filled, solid white shapes like the launcher's z. `material-icons-core` covers most; the few others (Pause, Replay 10, Forward 10, Subtitles, Space bar, Backspace) are copied into `NuageIcons` rather than pulling in `material-icons-extended`.

### Waiting (`PulsingDot`)
- The app's one sign of work in progress: an `ink` disc that breathes, shrinking to 40% of its size as it fades to 30% alpha and growing back, 700 ms each way on a fast-out, slow-in curve (#453: a fade alone read as a still dot on the stick, above all while a Sync holds back its frames). 6 dp beside a caption (the Catalog pill); 10 dp alone (the Sync checklist's running step, and the rows screens' wait after the page arrival time, Motion above). ### Running the prototypes
Debug builds include `PrototypeActivity`:

```
adb shell am start -n dev.betteriptv.debug/dev.betteriptv.app.prototype.PrototypeActivity --es screen <home|details|player|search|settings> [--ez focus true] [--ez tracks true]
```

`focus true` moves first focus from the main button to the first card (home) or the first Version (details); `tracks true` opens the player's Audio and subtitles cards. Prototypes carry a "Sample content" caption so nobody takes the made-up Versions for real ones. The design has only been checked on the emulator; checking it on the Fire TV Stick is tracked in victortrinh/better-iptv#23.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** read every colour, size, corner and gap from `Colors`, `Type`, `Corners`, `Spacing`, `Cards` and `FocusScale`. Screens never hold raw values.
- **Do** make every focusable thing a `FocusSurface`, or a component built on it, and pick its weight: Card for artwork, Control for buttons/tabs/keys/choices, Row for anything spanning a list.
- **Do** show Confirmed as a solid badge and Likely as an outlined badge everywhere a Version attribute appears: hero, card, details, player.
- **Do** put a full-bleed backdrop with `ground` scrims behind any screen that has a Title's artwork, and keep text on the scrimmed side.
- **Do** keep content inside the 48 x 27 dp overscan margins, applied as list `contentPadding`.
- **Do** check every text colour for 4.5:1 against what is behind it, including glass over artwork.
- **Do** size fixed widths for French strings about 20% longer, and cap single lines with an ellipsis.
- **Do** build on `androidx.tv:tv-material` (`Text`, `Icon`, `MaterialTheme` mapped by `NuageTheme`) and the components in `ui/components/`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add colour to the interface. No accent hue, tinted glass or coloured focus. Colour comes from artwork, Caution Amber marks cautions, and Failure Red marks failures, as text.
- **Don't** fill anything solid white unless it has focus.
- **Don't** use runtime blur (`Modifier.blur`, RenderEffect) for glass. On the Fire TV Stick glass is a translucent tint.
- **Don't** use Compose Material 3 phone components (`androidx.compose.material3`: Card, Button, TextField, NavigationBar and the like). They bring touch ripples, phone sizes and their own focus.
- **Don't** scale list rows on focus. They get a white fill or a white ring.
- **Don't** show a card's name or badges unless it has focus, and don't let the slot under a card collapse.
- **Don't** override EdgeScroll or scroll lists to a fixed pivot, outside Home's rows.
- **Don't** give buttons, tabs or rows a shadow. The lift belongs to focused artwork.
