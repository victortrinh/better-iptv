# A Movie is Watched in its last 10% or 10 minutes

A Movie counts as Watched, and leaves Continue watching, once the Viewer stops in its last 10% or its last 10 minutes, whichever is shorter, or plays it to the end. Netflix leaves a film in Continue watching until its credits start, using a credits marker it keeps for each film; neither the Provider nor TMDB gives us one, so the rule stands in for it. Credits of a two-hour film usually start 6 to 10 minutes from the end, which the earlier rule (5% or 8 minutes) missed. A flat 90%, as Plex does by default, would count a three-hour film finished 18 minutes early, in its last act. Reading the credits' start from the file, or learning it from Viewers who stop there, could replace the rule later.

Removing a Movie from Continue watching forgets where the Viewer stopped, unlike Netflix, which keeps it: for a household sharing one TV, removed should mean gone, and Play then starts it over.
