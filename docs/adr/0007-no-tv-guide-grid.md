# No TV guide grid

Live TV is reached through Favourites, search and the sports hub, not through a channel-by-time guide grid like TiviMate's. The Viewer mostly watches Movies and Shows, a fast grid over 45k Channels is a large piece of work on a small stick, and the Provider's guide covers only 15% of Channels with generic entries such as "Live: NHL Hockey". Sports are found from Event channel names and Game schedules instead.

## Amended 2026-11-01

Settled while designing M3. Live TV is also reached through a Channel list by Category: on the Live tab, a row of the Provider's countries, each opening its Categories beside their Channels, with what's on now and next beside a Channel where the Provider's guide knows it (asked per Channel, for the Channels on screen and the Favourites, not downloaded whole). The same now-and-next shows under a focused Favourite and in the player. That is TiviMate's groups-left, channels-right list without its time axis, and it is what lets the household stop using TiviMate. A channel-by-time grid remains out, for the reasons above: on this Provider 85% of its rows would be empty. Reconsider a grid over the Favourites alone if the household misses planning the evening across time.
